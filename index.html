01 — The problem

The rules exist. Applying them is hard.

Where the answers live today

Official road-rules pages. Accurate and authoritative — but spread across many pages and written in formal terms.

What new drivers need in the moment

“Can I park here at 6:30 pm?”
“What should I do at this roundabout?”
“I got a parking fine. What now?”
“Someone hit my parked car — what now?”
What if road rules were easier to learn through pictures, short videos, animations and practical examples — instead of long written explanations?That’s Motivo →

02 — Users & stakeholders

Who are we designing for?

🎓 First-time learners

Teens and adults learning signs, roundabouts, parking and safe-driving basics.

🔑 Learner & provisional drivers

Scenario practice, refreshers and clear licence conditions.

🕓 Returning drivers

A refresher on current rules, signs and parking after a long break.

🌏 International students & newcomers

Need to spot what’s different in NSW rules, parking and licensing.

🏠 Interstate movers

Can drive, but need NSW parking, tolls, fines, licence and crash rules.

Wider stakeholders

Need accurate information and links to legitimate support.

Driving instructors & schoolsCouncilsRoad-safety authoritiesInsurersOther road users

06 — Responsible AI & evaluation

Built to be trusted — and tested

Risks we must manage

RiskHow we reduce it
Wrong or outdated answerVerified NSW sources, citations shown, regular content review
Misread parking signShow uncertainty, ask for time and place, check nearby signs
Unsafe accident advicePre-reviewed safety-first checklist; emergencies go to 000
Privacy of photos and dataCollect only what’s needed; don’t keep photos unnecessarily
Over-reliance on AIShow limits; never replaces instructors, police or officials
